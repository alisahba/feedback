Mac how-to guides

Short, clear guides for capturing and sharing what's on your screen.

How to take a screenshot on a MacEvery way to take a screenshot on a Mac: the full screen, an area or a window, copying to the clipboard, changing where screenshots are saved, and a faster way with Feedback. How to annotate a screenshot on a MacHow to add arrows, boxes, circles and text to a screenshot on a Mac, using Preview's Markup or Feedback, and how to crop it and share it quickly. How to screen record on a MacHow to record your screen on a Mac with the built-in toolbar or QuickTime, how to stop a recording, and how to trim it and turn it into a GIF or MP4. How to convert a video to a GIF on a MacTurn any video or screen recording into a GIF on a Mac: right-click in Finder, or trim, crop and pick the size and frame rate to keep the file small. How to make a storyboard on a MacMake a storyboard on a Mac from screenshots, sketches or video frames: number and reorder frames, export the board as one image, and cut an animatic with timing and sound. A reference board app for Mac that floats on topCollect reference images on a free-form board and keep it floating above any app while you draw, design or edit. Set its opacity, let clicks pass through, and export a mood board. How to get a color palette from an image on a MacPick colors from anywhere on your Mac screen, pull a palette from an image, and export it as HEX, CSS, Tailwind, SwiftUI, design tokens or Adobe swatches.
